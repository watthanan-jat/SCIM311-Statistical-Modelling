What the course covers

Course overview

SCIM311 develops students' ability to explain and apply statistical modelling concepts, especially generalized linear models and time-series models, and to use those models appropriately in data analysis.

The first half of the semester introduces generalized linear models, response distributions, fitting, interpretation, and applications. After the midterm, the course turns to the structure of time-dependent data and the models used to analyse and forecast it.

Before midterm · Weeks 1–8

Generalized Linear Models

Concepts, response distributions, model fitting, interpretation, and applications.

After midterm · Weeks 10–16

Time-Series Models

Stationarity, ACF/PACF, MA, AR, ARMA, ARIMA, SARIMA, and forecasting.

What students will achieve

Course learning outcomes

All four outcomes cover the complete course.

CLO1

Explain GLM concepts

Describe generalized linear model concepts and select models for different response types.

CLO2

Apply generalized linear models

Apply GLMs to data analysis and interpret fitted models in statistical and practical terms.

CLO3

Analyse stationary time series

Explain time-series concepts and analyse stationarity, autocorrelation, ACF/PACF, and MA, AR, and ARMA models.

CLO4

Diagnose and forecast

Forecast time-series outcomes using ARIMA/SARIMA models and the Box–Jenkins model-building and diagnostic workflow.
